### @chrisbbh

Not getting paid but I generally recommend it when talking to friends. 

First, cloud agents are just more scalable. I have 4-5 computers running, but dealing with maintenance and OOM from too many agents or dev processes is just annoying. Agents are noisy neighbors, so running them on one machine can slow them down.

But I also run a bunch of agents on my own VPS(s). Some projects need specific infra or very custom setups that you can’t really get with cloud agents. 
Here’s also the case for getting your own computer. You could do that, but the specs of a good one makes it costly. You can’t just spin it down at no cost when it’s not used anymore. And when you rent, you can easily upgrade as it suits your needs. 
Winning a good server in a Hetzner auction is a pretty good deal.

And hardware depreciate. I’m also using my old gaming desktop, but it’s just not that powerful anymore. And it has the occasional fault from being used a lot. But it’s pretty neat to use as a Linux server with wake-on-LAN, so if anyone has old hardware around that’s also fine to use.
