### @kunchenguid

be very careful if you find yourself simply passing messages to and from your agents

e.g. people ask you a question on slack, and you simply ask an agent and pass its response back - it should be obvious that your job can be automated in that case

instead, you should try and build an agent (or sometimes just skills) that can reliably answer similar questions moving forward and remove yourself from the loop

your value then becomes improving said agents and skills, and your impact can easily scale and manifest even when you sleep

don’t be automated. be the automator
