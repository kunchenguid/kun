### @kunchenguid

sharing an emotional milestone plus some tips for growing a social media presence

if you came across my youtube channel, you might have seen i always had “ex-meta” or “L8 principal” mentioned in the title of my videos

i hated doing it, and every time i published a video i always a/b tested removing that title

unfortunately, every single time, removing it would destroy the reach and the videos simply wouldn’t be seen nearly as much

the latest video was the first one i ever had where the “ex-Meta L8” title no longer made any difference. and i have now picked the variant that’s just “with Kun”

i’m _so_ happy to see it. it’s hard to detach from an identity that was built up over decades of experience. this video is the milestone that tells me i have now created my own identity over the past couple of months after quitting the big tech game

the learning i internalized for growing a social media presence is that everyone’s busy. no one’s going to spend time following content that they don’t know is good or not

the exact same tweet, the exact same video, when published by someone the audience trusts, will always get more engagement than when it’s shared by someone without proven credibility

so to establish a new presence, my theory is that you need to figure out how to get people to recognize and trust you. that’s the real work, not learning all kinds of engagement baits which might work in the short term to a degree but doesn’t help you build a durable and trusted reputation

people do give some trust to authority and experience, so if you have that, you can use it to accelerate the cold start. otherwise, i suggest you focus on creating things that are genuinely useful for others. things, not opinions. an opinion from a random person is the most noisy thing on social media that no one has time for

keep showing up with cool stuff that others can actually benefit from, you’ll gradually establish your identity
