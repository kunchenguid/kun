### @mrgadgetstudio

I think Andrej’s prompting rules are excellent and very well thought out. But I’m not convinced prompting will play that big a role in how we use AI going forward.

For coding projects, I think project architecture and how you organize files so agents can quickly understand the codebase matter far more than prompt wording, most models already understand what we’re trying to achieve. In other areas like video, image generation, and analysis, prompting may matter more today, but those workflows are increasingly being handled by agents. Eventually, the bots may just talk to each other and orchestrate the work themselves, without us writing prompts at all.
