@kunchenguid Totally. I think Personal Content has two points. First, the act of creating is fulfilling for the individual. Second, Personal Content does not mean you never share it with the world. With 8B people out there, there are bound to be others enjoying it. 

For example, I used to tell my kids stories about Felix the Yucky Cat, who lived in a garbage can. I made these up on the fly. With AI, I can now animate them, turn them into a book, make short videos about them. My kids will love that. The writing remains original, the art becomes far more accessible. And maybe others appreciate it too.

Personal Content also won't displace mass market content. It will compliment it!
