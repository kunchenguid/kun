### @kunchenguid

before agents, when you get a new idea, it's almost always better to put it into a backlog, then occasionally prioritize the backlog to make sure you are working on the most impactful thing

with agents, it's almost always better to get it done right away when the idea is still fresh, when you still have the right memory and context about the real intent and requirements

i think many experienced product builders will have to go through this transition - it's very counter-intuitive
