### @CalvinGrunewald

New blog post is out! This provides a good example of how I work these days on a piece of low-consequence, personal software. I cover two concepts that might be interesting to you:

1. Shotgun prompting: the idea here is to get what you want with a lot of short, direct fast prompts that are mostly subconscious (thinking fast instead of slow) so you can focus on other things. 

2. Building the system that lets an agent drive: the idea here is to build in a mechanism to make it very easy for your agents to use the software. This is different than embedding an agent.

Stet is a markdown editor - it's like iaWriter, but free and has a bunch of features I want (like posting to Substack, vim keybindings, and suggestion editing). I hope the techniques I used to build it are useful, or maybe you find Stet useful too!
