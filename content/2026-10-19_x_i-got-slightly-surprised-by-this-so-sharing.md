### @kunchenguid

i got slightly surprised by this so sharing in case it’s helpful -

after unifying your X subscription, your Grok Bot is no longer running on its own quota like before! it’s now sharing the same weekly quota as coding

this is actually a good thing - previously the grok bot quota was a bit tight and now it draws from the larger pool - but you’ll need to plan for this or otherwise may end up like me and used up all the quota for coding and got surprised why grok bot stopped working

my plan is to start using grok bot as the ONLY interface for my X subscription quota. i will no longer use my X sub from Pi and other harnesses

my grok bot has become increasingly capable as i replicated my firstmate there https://t.co/KC0bhgoPMi and for most of my open source maintenance work, i find almost no reason to do them locally now

the combination of an always-on VM, automated routines, cursor cloud agents, and continuously self-improving bots is quite a powerful combination of primitives for doing pretty much everything
