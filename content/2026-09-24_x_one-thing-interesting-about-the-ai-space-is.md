### @kunchenguid

one thing interesting about the AI space is that it’s not very easy to create a network effect

with social apps, every user makes the app itself more valuable. you can make a slightly better whatsapp - you still can’t get users to move because all their contacts are already there

with AI being used mostly in a private setting, there isn’t as much opportunity for network effect to kick in. users can easily switch if they found something better or cheaper

as a result, being early and building a big user base doesn’t help secure a moat. a better product can easily take all the users away

whoever figures out a way to establish strong network effect on AI products will win big
