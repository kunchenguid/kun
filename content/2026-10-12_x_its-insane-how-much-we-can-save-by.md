### @kunchenguid

it's insane how much we can save by leveraging our own computers

a few weeks ago i saw my github actions bill was starting to cost, so i started running all mac CI jobs with my self-hosted runners on my m4 mac mini

i just calculated - if i used one of the CI providers for all those mac CI runs, it would have been a $1500-$3000/month bill

absolutely crazy that:

- my self-hosted runners didn't even use half of my mac mini's capacity, yet the equivalent from cloud providers for just a month of usage is more expensive than buying the mac mini itself

- this is way higher than my LLM bills. computers are going to be the real expensive part of software development, not AI
