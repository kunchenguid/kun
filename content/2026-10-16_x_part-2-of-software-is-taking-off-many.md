### @kunchenguid

part 2 of "software is taking off"

many people argued - now that everyone will just have their agent build whatever they need, software businesses will no longer be able to make money, hence "software is dead"

i'm here to show you why that's wrong, by exploring what happened to major technological transformations in the past

fig 1. microwave vs restaurants

in the 1980s, the adoption of microwave in households started to explode. it allowed pretty much anyone to cook a simple meal for themselves in a matter of minutes

many predicted that the restaurant businesses will collapse because why pay for expensive restaurant dining when it's so easy to make food at home

now with the benefit of hindsight, we can see the adoption of microwave did not slow down restaurant business growth AT ALL - not by a single bit

fig 2. espresso machines vs coffee shops

similarly to the above, espresso machines gained significant adoption since 2000

intuitively, every cup of coffee people make at home is a few dollars the coffee shops would lose, so the growth of espresso machines and coffee shops should go in opposite direction

but they did not. they both grew at the same time

fig 3. iphone camera vs photographers

when everyone has a camera in their pocket all the time, it would surely put photographers out of their jobs, right? ...right?

counter to many people's intuition, employment of professional photographers remained stable after iphone adoption exploded in the 2010s

there was a little dip due to COVID, but it's recovered since, and overall it even grew slightly since 2000

fig 4. tiktok vs streaming

short form videos absolutely exploded in growth since its introduction. tiktok allowed pretty much everyone to easily create entertaining videos others can watch

as the incumbent entertainment video format, TVs and movies would totally take a massive hit, right?

but no it didn't

how?? all these industries got democratized by technology and became accessible to a much larger number of people, why won't they just die, like the hype trains said they would?

you see - 

people go to coffee shops and restaurants to be lazy, to be serviced, to socialize, to celebrate. you can't do any of that with a microwave and espresso machine at home

people hire photographers for their wedding because it's a special day and they want the beautifully captured memory to stand out in their album. using a phone is the opposite of special

people watch tv shows and movies to enjoy a well crafted story, feel moved, touched, and sometimes to share some emotion and bond with others. tiktok scrolling does not give them that at all

when technology is democratized and made accessible to the general public, they tend to be used to create a different set of things, not a replacement to the existing professional business

go to your phone settings and take a look at the apps where you spent the most time. for me, that's X, discord, wechat, whatsapp, youtube, youtube music, tiktok

your agent can't create a replacement for any single one of them. look at yours and ask yourself which one would you bother to vibe code?

what regular people will create with AI is going to be an exciting set of new things. things that never existed before, that would open up new markets and be net new to the economy. things that are way more interesting than "you built your kanban board, and i did one too"
