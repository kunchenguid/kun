a few things here -

1. yes it's widely agreed that longer requests will see degraded recall, however they don't affect task performance the way people think they do. the vast majority of real world tasks aren't needle-in-haystack retrieval tasks

2. none of these reports match a magic number "300k" - that's my biggest problem with these statements because they tell people to use a static threshold but in practice it depends a lot on the model and tasks. the degradation is not discrete at a certain threshold - it's continuous across the spectrum
