My biggest astra tip is that you have to be ambitious and clear in the outcome you expect.

This is not a model you sit and chat with. It's a model that you say "build me the universe" and it goes off and does it.

Astra is not ambitious. It requires explicit goals.

### @kunchenguid

this is actually a big problem i have with some of the newer models. opus 5 was the same

in real world usage, there isn’t so many “ambitious goals” we can just throw to agents in a way that actually makes economic sense

none of those 3d fps games people vibe code can actually make nearly enough money compared to the tokens they burn

practical real world tasks often need human in the loop to provide judgment and steering to generate valuable outcomes

fable is so good because it can go off on long horizon tasks, and also sit down and talk nicely if needed

we can have both long horizon and human in the loop capabilities, and we shouldn’t give up either
