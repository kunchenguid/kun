### @kunchenguid

been testing the new multiplexer Rex for a while, and my feelings are very mixed

tl;dr i think i'll stick with herdr for now

while it's a beautifully designed app, i couldn't quite find any real reason yet to prefer Rex other than it being beautiful

biggest sticking point is that it's a middle ground between GUI and TUI. with a full on GUI like t3code we get rich visuals and interactions. with a full on TUI like tmux and herdr we get pure keyboard interaction and can run it from any ssh client consistently

with Rex i get neither of that. and i have to use Rex everywhere or otherwise i'm locked out of my own work. if i'm going to abandon some of the benefits of full TUI over simple ssh, i'd probably just go full GUI at that point, instead of locking my terminal sessions behind another GUI app

this also makes me question the new herdr app, because it seems to be heading in the same direction as Rex. they will both end up competing with other GUI apps without the strength of being a real terminal program

i hope herdr TUI stays because that's still my favorite. perhaps turn it into a client for herdr-core so people can choose to access the same sessions from either a GUI or a TUI?
