it’s really difficult to love google’s AI effort right now

their subscription terms is the most hostile one to the ecosystem among all the labs

if you subscribe to google AI, you can pretty much only use antigravity. they require all prompts to antigravity to come directly from the user, not an orchestrator or loop. many users got banned for using it in other ADEs and orchestrators

claude, codex, grok - they are all a lot more flexible in all kinds of integrations

there’s only one reason google needs to be this restrictive about requiring direct user prompts to antigravity - it’s harvesting user data for model training

but it’s not really panning out, is it? their models have been improving but still far behind. as of now, there’s just no practical reason to use any of the latest gemini models when i have opus and fable

relying so heavily on data collection also indicates their training pipeline is still in the last era of SFT and they haven’t actually nailed RLVR like the other labs have

i think google really needs to revisit their AI strategy. this is just not working
