@kunchenguid, would you say that backpass and /stow two peas in the same pod, so to speak? One for agents.md in general (and retroactive for past sessions), while the other is specifically for firstmate (and only for the current session)?

### @kunchenguid

yes! here's the full story:

1. initially i relied on agents writing memory, both automatically and prompted by me

2. then i found letting agents write memory automatically was not working well. this includes claude's "auto memory" feature which i turned off. there's way too much noise, anecdotes, staleness happening

3. so i resorted to asking agents to write down learnings when i know there's something valuable to document. this evolved into "/stow" in firstmate. but to share the idea with people who aren't using firstmate, i created a standalone "/stow" skill as well

4. after lots of real world usage from many users, i realized people are confused about when to use "/stow". it's hard to get it right. this pushed me to think hard about what a more systematic solution looks like

5. that led to a lot of time researching and exploring potential systema, and resulted in backpass

from my own usage, backpass is hands down the best way i can find right now to keep agent memory and skills effective and efficient. but it's still on my plate to make it integrate well in firstmate, hence the duality at the moment
